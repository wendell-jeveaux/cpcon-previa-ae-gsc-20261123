Frequently asked questions

What is RFID Technology and how does it work for inventory tracking?

RFID is essentially a tracking technology that utilizes from radio waves to identify objects. As RFID tags can be read wirelessly and without line-of-sight, inventory items can be tracked continuously as they move throughout the supply chain or within a facility. Supply Chain tasks, such as receiving, picking, and replenishing can be streamlined and automated.

Do I need to replace my existing WMS or ERP system to implement RFID tracking?

No! RFID data can be integrated to with your existing systems via APIs, allowing for seamless integration and data exchange. Enhancing inventory visibility, real-time updates, and improved accuracy.

Can RFID help in reducing manual labor and improving operational efficiency in supply chain management?

RFID enables automated data capture without the need for manual scanning or data entry. By simply passing tagged items through RFID readers, the technology automatically collects and records item-specific information, such as product details, quantities, and locations. This eliminates the time-consuming manual process of individually scanning barcodes or manually updating inventory records RFID expedites the receiving and shipping processes within the supply chain. As items are equipped with RFID tags, they can be automatically identified and matched against purchase orders or shipping manifests. This eliminates manual verification processes and accelerates the flow of goods, reducing bottlenecks and enhancing operational efficiency.

How does RFID inventory tracking help in reducing stockouts and overstocks?

By providing real-time visibility of inventory levels and movement, RFID technology enables businesses to proactively manage their stock. The high accuracy of RFID tracking minimizes errors in stock counting and improves inventory record accuracy, contributing to better decision-making.
