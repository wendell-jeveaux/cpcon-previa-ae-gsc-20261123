Today CPCON utilizes from a global network to help clients enhance internal control, financial supervision and risk management through offices in the Americas, Europe and Middle East.

Network Governance

CPCON Group is a global advisory firm that provides clients with risk management and financial advisory solutions, under the name of CPCON. Each CPCON Member firm is a member of the CPCON International Limited, a US company limited by guarantee, either as a voting or non-voting member.

To facilitate communication, coordination of actions and the sharing of best practice, CPCON is divided into three regions: EMEA (Europe, Middle East and Sub-Saharan Africa) and Americas (Latin America, North America and Caribbean), each supported by a Regional Board.
