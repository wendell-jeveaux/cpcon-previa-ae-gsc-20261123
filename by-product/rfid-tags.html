RFID TAGS FOR ASSET TRACKING

CPCON offers many types of passive UHF labels for asset management, along with custom printing and encoding services. Our RFID smart labels help ensure seamless traceability and reduce losses, while enhancing asset tracking and data collection speed (and accuracy). See our RFID product line of asset labels including flexible, printable and long-reading options in all sizes.

ASSETCODE ON

Format: UHF RFID Label
Type: RFID Asset Label (On-Metal)
RF Protocol: ISO18000-6C
Reading Distance: 10-ft / 3 meters (on-metal and off-metal surfaces)
Properties: Printable and Flexible
Size (dimensions): 2.55 x 1.35″ (inches) / 65 x 35 mm
Attachment: Sticker Tag
Custom Options: Pre-Encoding + Printing (Barcode, Logo, Qr code, etc.)

ASSETCODE OFF

Format:UHF RFID Label
Type:RFID Asset Label (Off-Metal)
RF Protocol:ISO18000-6C
Reading Distance:10-ft / 3 meters (on-metal and off-metal surfaces)
Properties:Printable and Flexible
Size (dimensions):3.11 x 0.78″ (inches) / 79 x 20 mm
Attachment:Sticker Tag
Custom Options: Pre-Encoding + Printing (Barcode, Logo, Qr code, etc.)
