Top Asset Management challenges for maintenance practices

Downtimes

Manual data collection processes to identify equipment are time-consuming during walkdowns and routine inspections.

High Costs Associated with Planning

Little or no data on equipment and maintenance activities makes it difficult to manage operations planning and maintenance spend.

Little CMMS Visibility

Constant equipment movement and asset disposals affects the quality of descriptive and locational information, reflecting on data gaps in the maintenance operations

Asset Identification

Gather complex equipment information with Bulk RFID Scan to speed up and automate data collection/entry processes. RFID tags can be read and located from up to 12-ft distance.

Asset Management

Use RFID handheld to locate and uncover asset information – without relying on scrapping nameplates, and write data to RFID Tag to record repair and re-certification notes in real-time.

Integrated CMMS-RFID Visibility

Create CMMS interface or integration to drive business process efficiencies in data input and asset visibility.

The data store on the RFID Equipment Tag can be exported to your CMMS to give you a complete view of your operation. Enabling you to record maintenance activities and update recertification dates.
